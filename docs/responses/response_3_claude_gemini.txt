Here's what's packed into it:
Physics & Game Loop

Timer.periodic at ~60fps (16ms ticks)
Gravity accumulates each frame; tap fires an instant velocity impulse
Bird rotation angle clamps between -45° (climbing) and +90° (nosediving)

Graphics (pure CustomPainter / widgets)

Bird — gradient oval body, wing bezier, layered eye with shine, triangular beak
Pipes — linear gradient green with a wider cap and a shadow stripe for depth
Ground — scrolling striped grass on a dirt base using the ground offset counter
Clouds — three overlapping ovals at fixed decorative positions

Collision & Scoring

Pixel-precise AABB collision with a small forgiveness margin (6px) so it feels fair
Score increments when the bird's center X crosses a pipe center (one-shot flag via an extension map)
Best score tracked across restarts in the same session

UI States

Idle — title card + pulsing animated "TAP TO START" button
Playing — large outlined score counter at top
Dead — frosted card overlay with SCORE / BEST side-by-side + pulsing restart hint
